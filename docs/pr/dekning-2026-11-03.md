# Dekning: universe and levels, the after-report loop, one kill list (D38–D40)

Three parts on one branch, each with its decision in `DECISIONS.md` and tests on synthetic data (the Toolkit side –
the case factory and the kill-list export – is `equity-research-toolkit` branch `new-case`).

## 1. The coverage universe (D38) – `dashboard/coverage.py`, `oq coverage`
Every company in the store gets a level derived from the files: *full* (report published or model file in the book),
*light* (light case), *watch* (watchlist), *none*. The plan – the level a company should be at, who owns the view,
the rhythm – is the only thing typed in (`~/osloquant-home/coverage.yaml`, `oq coverage set`). The coverage page shows
the universe by sector group (64 companies: 3 full, 15 light, 46 none today), the gap to the plan, stale light cases,
and keeps the two-analyst table. The front page carries the counts.

## 2. After the report (D39) – `dashboard/afterreport.py`, `oq case after-report [--due]`
The newest report is found from four sources (reader summary, downloaded report, research-book event, financial
calendar). The loop re-records the statements, rebuilds the light case with before/after, fetches the report from
NewsWeb, reads it with the spec or dry-runs a generic spec and writes a suggested one (`cases/specs/`). The queue of
who owes a new view is derived, not stored; Oscar's items reach the front page (D29), Claude's the coverage page
(D31). Needed on the way: `period_of` now reads "Third quarter 2026", "3Q 2026", "3. kvartal 2026", "half-year";
the key-row parser accepts the five-column layout without printed changes, with a plausibility filter. Elkem's Q3
report is read this way (revenue 3 587, EBITDA 554, NIBD 6 459 found). `oq case export CODE` writes the statements
for the Toolkit's case factory.

## 3. One kill list (D40) – `dashboard/killlist.py`, `oq book check [--stub]`
The model's list, exported with the report, is compared with the view's sentences and the reader's rules; every
divergence is named on the stock page, in data health and in the CLI. Nothing is rewritten.
