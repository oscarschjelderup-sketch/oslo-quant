Do technical buy signals work on Oslo Børs?

Measured 2010-01-01 to 2026-10-19 on 64 stocks listed today. Entry at the next day's open; excess return = the stock minus the equal-weighted average of all of them over the same days.

The answer

No buy signal pays after costs. The best of them, “Breakout 60d high + volume”, beat the equal-weighted market by +0.4% over the next 20 sessions on average – less than the ~0.5% a round trip costs – and was right 48% of the time.

The risk side carries more information: after “Falling channel entry” the stock trailed the market by -1.4% over the next 60 sessions on average.

So the signals are not a strategy. In this project they are used only as timing and risk alerts on companies with a written fundamental case, and every alert shows the odds below.
