Play money. Three paper accounts from the oslo-quant store, NOK 100 000 each, on Oslo Børs. Quotes are Yahoo Finance's, about 15 minutes late, and the 15-minute bars skip the closing auction. An order fills at the open of the next bar plus 0.1 % slippage; commission is Nordnet Mini's 0.15 % with a NOK 29 minimum. A missed look is a missed signal: the looks are made from a laptop that sleeps. Public holidays are not known to the market clock.

Nothing here is advice and nothing can reach a broker. The troll's verdicts are commentary on the books, written by Claude to the charter in TROLL.md. A rewrite forks the goat's account: the old one keeps grazing in the herd, the new one starts with its value.
